Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Nature Core AI, we collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Usage data: Questions asked, plants searched, features used
  • Payment data: Processed securely by Paystack — we never store card details
  • Device data: Browser type, device type, IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Nature Core AI platform
  • To manage your account and subscription
  • To personalize your experience
  • To send important account notifications
  • To improve our AI responses and plant database

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with encryption at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

4. AI Conversations

Questions you ask Nature Core AI may be used to improve our AI system and plant database. We do not share individual conversations publicly. Conversations are processed through Groq AI infrastructure.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Database and authentication
  • Groq AI — AI response generation
  • Paystack — Payment processing
  • Render — Backend hosting

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data at any time
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Export your data in a readable format

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Children

Nature Core AI is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes via email.
